✦ Our Story

A meadow built by friends

Lavender Meadows is a warm, welcoming Minecraft SMP where creativity blooms and friendships grow. Whether you're a builder, farmer, adventurer, or just love hanging out — there's a place here for you.

We believe Minecraft is best when it's cozy. No toxic vibes, just good people, beautiful builds, and endless fields of lavender.

✦ Server Rules

Rules & Strike System

To be here we ask you follow our rules and Discord's official ToS. Rules are broken into offence tiers.

⚠️

First Offence → Warning

R1, R3, R5, R8, R11, R12, R13

⏳

Second Offence → Timeout

R4, R6, R10

🚫

Third Offence → Ban

R7, R2, R9, R14

  • R1
    No Disrespect, Racism or Hate Warning

    No disrespect of any kind towards staff or other players. Keep this a kind and fun environment.

  • R2
    No Violence Immediate Ban

    Absolutely no violence. No threats of death, no imagery or media depicting or promoting violence, self-harm, or harm to anyone.

  • R3
    Communicate! Warning

    If you have an issue with staff, players or the game please communicate. It can't be resolved without asking for help.

  • R4
    No NSFW Timeout / Ban

    Zero tolerance for NSFW or explicit imagery, media or conversations. Respect that there are minors here.

  • R5
    Voice Chat Etiquette Warning

    Be respectful in voice channels. Don't yell, involve everyone, and make room for shyer members.

  • R6
    No Unapproved Promotion or Advertising Timeout

    If you want to promote something, ask a mod first — they'll run it by the Owners. Promoting without permission results in a timeout.

  • R7
    No Illegal Activity Immediate Ban

    Acting or speaking about involving yourself or others in illegal activities will result in an immediate ban.

  • R8
    No Spam or Mass Pinging Warning / Mute

    Refrain from spamming or mass pinging people in the server.

  • R9
    No Sexual Behaviour Towards Minors Immediate Ban

    Absolutely zero sexual comments to anybody — especially minors. This results in an immediate ban.

  • R10
    Age Role Required Warning → Timeout

    Select your age role in Discord. Without it you'll receive a reminder, then a warning, then a timeout.

  • R11
    No Excessive Foul Language Warning

    You're allowed to swear, but be mindful — not everyone is comfortable with excessive cursing in chat or VC.

  • R12
    Be Smart Warning

    If you think it's rude, don't say or do it. Use common sense — not everything needs to be said.

  • R13
    Soundboard Use Warning

    Soundboards are monitored. All VC rules apply — no slurs, inappropriate sounds, or spamming.

  • R14
    No Slurs Immediate Ban

    Slurs of any kind will not be tolerated, regardless of who you are or any claim of permission to say them.
